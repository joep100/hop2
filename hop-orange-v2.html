How hop works

Simple for you. Seamless for your customer.

Four steps, and the shop does two of them. Nothing to install, no app, no developer and no commission on the sale.

1

Add it to your checkout

A delivery option in settings you already own. Shopify, WooCommerce, Wix, Squarespace or a site somebody built you.

Three minutes, once.

2

Tell us when it is ready

An order in is not an order ready. You tell us on a link when it is packed — an Eircode, a name and a mobile number.

About thirty seconds, from anywhere.

3

We collect at 2pm

A rider calls to you at the same time every day, Monday to Friday. Whatever is packed and waiting goes out.

Nothing to arrange or ring in.

4

We deliver by 6pm

Deliveries run from 2pm to 6pm. Across our September rounds the median drop was 3.20pm, and 99% had arrived by 5.54pm.

Your customer gets a tracking text.

Your own stock, delivered today.

The thing your customer wants is already on your shelf, two kilometres from their door. We are the bit in between — our own riders, on our own bikes, crossing this city all day anyway.

What it costs

You set what the customer pays. We charge you ours, and you keep the difference.

We charge you from€9.95

Per delivery, plus VAT, whatever is in the bag. Sandyford, Tallaght, Dún Laoghaire and Swords cost a little more, and we will confirm your rates when we speak. Most shops charge one price at the checkout whatever the address — usually around €14.99 — so same-day is a line they earn on rather than a cost they absorb. Billed monthly on what actually went, and nothing at all in a month where you send nothing.
