Setting up local delivery in Shopify

Seven steps in your own settings, about five minutes. No app to install, nothing to pay Shopify.

If you are not the person who looks after your website

Forward this page to whoever is. It takes about five minutes and there is nothing to install.

Two of the decisions are yours rather than theirs, so tell them before they start: what time you will tell customers and what you will charge. Both are below.

Before you start

Decide what time you will tell customers. A rider calls to you at 2pm, so pick a time that leaves you enough of a gap to pack. 1pm is the usual answer.

Decide what you will charge. Most shops land near €14.99. Ours is €9.95 plus VAT for most of Dublin and €12.95 for Sandyford, Tallaght, Dún Laoghaire and Swords. You charge one price whatever the address, so set it with that in mind.

Seven steps

1

Open your delivery settings

In Shopify admin, go to Settings, then Shipping and delivery.

2

Find Local delivery, further down the page

Scroll past the shipping zones. Local delivery is its own section near the bottom.

3

Turn it on and set the area

Choose your location, then Use postal codes rather than a radius. Paste the list below.

Paste this listD01*, D02*, D03*, D04*, D05*, D06*, D6W*, D07*, D08*, D09*, D10*, D11*, D12*, D13*, D14*, D15*, D16*, D17*, D18*, D20*, D22*, D24*, A94*, A96*, K36*, K67*, K78*

The asterisks matter. They tell Shopify to match every Eircode beginning with those characters.

4

Put in your price

Set what the customer pays. One figure, whatever the address.

5

Write the wording in Delivery information

Shopify calls the option “Local delivery” and you cannot rename it, so the cut-off goes here instead.

Suggested wordingSame-day delivery in Dublin. Order by 1pm and it arrives between 2pm and 6pm today. Orders after 1pm go the next working day.
6

Save

Click Save. It is live immediately.

7

Ring us once it is live

Ring 01 425 5722. We add your shop to the 2pm collection round and send you the link your staff use to book each delivery.

Nothing will be collected until you have done this, so do not skip it.

One thing Shopify will not do for you

There is no cut-off setting, so the option shows at every hour of the day. Somebody ordering at five in the afternoon will still see it. That is why the cut-off belongs in the delivery information, where they cannot miss it.

An order after 1pm is not a lost order either. It would not be collected by a standard carrier until the next day anyway, so on hop it still reaches the customer sooner.

Check it worked

Go to your own shop as a customer would, put anything in the basket and enter a Dublin address. Your same-day option should appear. Now change it to a Bray address, which starts A98, and it should disappear.

Later: letting Shopify send us the orders

Once you are booking enough that typing each order is a nuisance, Shopify can send them to us by itself and we write the tracking number back onto the order. That is a job for whoever looks after the website, and the instructions are separate: hopshop.ie/connected. There is no hurry.

If you get stuck

Ring 01 425 5722 and we will walk you through it on the phone. It takes about three minutes and we would far rather do that than have you spend twenty minutes on it.
