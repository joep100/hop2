Questions shops ask

Including the ones with awkward answers. If something is not covered here, ring 01 425 5722 and ask.

Money

What does it cost us?

From €9.95 plus VAT per delivery, whatever is in the bag. Sandyford, Tallaght, Dún Laoghaire and Swords cost €12.95, and we confirm your rates when we speak. Billed monthly on what actually went.

Is there a minimum, or a monthly fee?

No. No contract, no minimum, no joining fee. In a month where you send nothing, you pay nothing.

Do you take a cut of the sale?

No. A delivery charge only. The sale and the customer stay yours.

What should we charge the customer?

That is yours to set. Most shops land around €14.99 and charge one price whatever the address, which means same-day is a line you earn on rather than a cost you absorb.

The day

What is the cut-off?

An order placed before 1pm goes out on the same day. Anything after that goes on the next working day.

When do you collect?

2pm, Monday to Friday, the same time every day. Whatever is packed and waiting goes out, and there is nothing to arrange or ring in.

When does the customer get it?

Between 2pm and 6pm. Across our September rounds the median drop was 3.20pm and 99% had arrived by 5.54pm. They get a text with a tracking link once the rider has it.

What about Saturdays?

We run Monday to Friday. Orders placed after 1pm on Friday go out on Monday afternoon.

Setting it up

Do we need a developer?

No. It is a delivery option in settings you already own, and it takes about three minutes. We will do it with you on the phone if you would rather.

Does it work with our platform?

Shopify, WooCommerce, Wix, Squarespace or a site somebody built you — every one of them can add a delivery method. We have written instructions for Shopify and WooCommerce.

Is there an app to install?

No. Nothing to install, nothing to pay anybody else, and nothing that can break when your platform updates.

How do we book each order?

On a link we give you. An Eircode, a name and a mobile number, about thirty seconds. It works on a phone, so it does not depend on anybody being in the shop.

When something goes wrong

Who does the customer blame?

Us, and we answer the phone. Our own riders, thirty-eight years in this city, and a person at the end of 01 425 5722.

What if nobody is in?

The rider rings the mobile number on the booking. If there is still nobody there we bring it back and come to an arrangement with you — we will not leave it on a doorstep unless you have told us to.

What if the address is outside the area?

The booking screen tells your staff before they take the customer’s details. Ring us anyway — we can often do it, just not automatically.

Can we cancel a booking?

Yes, before the rider collects. Ring us and we will pull it.

Something not covered here?

Ring 01 425 5722 and ask. It is usually a two-minute conversation, and we would rather have it than have you wondering.
